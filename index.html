THE PROBLEM

Space is hard on the human body.

NASA groups the dangers of spaceflight into five hazards. They don't stay separate, either: each one can make the others worse.

Radiation

Invisible, and one of the most hazardous parts of spaceflight.

Isolation

Small crew, small space, for months or years.

Distance

Mars is about 140 million miles away. Help is far off.

Gravity

Muscles, bones, inner ear and organs all have to adjust.

Closed environment

Air, temperature, noise and microbes all need watching.

Source: NASA HRP · 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight

MODULE 01 · EXERCISES

The daily workout, step by step.

Every astronaut exercises during their mission to slow the loss of bone, muscle and heart fitness. This tab is that plan.

  • 9 exercises: strength on ARED, cardio on the T2 treadmill and CEVIS bike, plus balance drills
  • Each one has how-to steps, the muscles it works, load and rest times, and why it matters up there
  • Adjust the sets, mark exercises done and track today's progress
  • A built-in timer loads the right length for each exercise
Strength · Lower body25 min · 90s rest

Back Squat on ARED

Without gravity your hips and spine stop carrying load and start losing bone. Heavy squats put that load back.

1Set the bar pads across your upper back and clip your feet into the platform.
2Sit your hips back and down until your thighs are about parallel.
3Push through your whole foot to stand back up.

MODULE 03 · SCENARIOS

15 situations crews train for.

Each one says what the crew member is going through, why it happens in space, and gives a checklist to work through.

  • Critical: the bends, ammonia leak, neck-vein clot, appendicitis, solar storm
  • Moderate: SANS vision changes, CO₂ headaches, kidney stone, shingles-type rash, a struggling crew member, fainting at landing
  • Routine: motion sickness, something in the eye, fingernail damage, poor sleep
  • Search, filter by severity, and track progress through each checklist
MED-02 // SPACEWALK

Shoulder pain after a spacewalk

About 35 minutes after a 6-hour spacewalk, EV1 has a deep, aching pain in the right shoulder that's getting worse.

✓Put them on 100% oxygen straight away.
✓Quick neuro check: speech, pupils, grip, coordination.
✓Call the ground and start treatment pressure per flight rules.
✓Give fluids by mouth if they can drink.

Source: NASA HRP · Risks of Human Spaceflight · Ongoing HRP Research

WHY IT MATTERS

Built for orbit.
Useful on Earth.

What happens to astronauts looks a lot like faster aging and the effects of sitting too much. Six months in space gives data that would take years to collect on the ground.

Health on Earth

Space research has helped with balance problems, osteoporosis, heart and blood vessel disorders, and type 2 diabetes.

Radiation tech

CANS-style detectors could also be used in cancer therapy, protecting aircrews, and nuclear threat detection.

Under the hood
